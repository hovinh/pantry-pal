=== Broccoli Pesto Pasta ===
Folder  : broccoli_pesto
Cuisine : Italian
Servings: 2
Tags    : vegetarian, pasta, quick

Description:
1. Cook pasta in well-salted boiling water until al dente. In the last 4 minutes, add all the broccoli florets to the same pot. Reserve 120 ml pasta water, then drain both together.
2. Set aside one-third of the broccoli florets for texture. Transfer the rest to a food processor.
3. Add pine nuts, garlic, Parmesan, lemon juice, and olive oil to the processor. Blitz until a coarse pesto forms, adding a splash of pasta water to loosen if needed. Season with salt and black pepper.
4. Return pasta and reserved florets to the pot over low heat. Spoon in the broccoli pesto and toss well, adding reserved pasta water a little at a time until the sauce coats every strand.
5. Serve immediately, topped with extra Parmesan, a drizzle of olive oil, and fresh basil leaves.

Ingredients:
  - pasta: 200 g
  - broccoli: 300 g
  - pine_nuts: 30 g
  - parmesan_cheese: 40 g
  - garlic: 1 cloves / 5 g
  - olive_oil: 40 g
  - lemon: 15 g
  - basil: 10 g
  - salt: 0.5 tsp
  - black_pepper: 0.25 tsp

=== Canned Sardine Stir-Fried with Tomato ===
Folder  : canned_sardine_tomato
Cuisine : Asian
Servings: 2
Tags    : main course, quick, pantry-friendly, seafood

Description:
1. Drain the canned sardines. If using whole sardines, roughly break them into large chunks.
2. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sliced onion; sauté for 3 minutes until softened.
3. Add canned tomato and stir. Cook for 5 minutes until the sauce deepens in colour.
4. Add sardines, soy sauce, sugar, chili flakes, and black pepper. Stir gently to combine without breaking up the fish too much.
5. Simmer for 3–4 minutes until the sauce is slightly reduced.
6. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve over steamed rice.

Ingredients:
  - canned_sardine: 200 g
  - canned_tomato: 200 g
  - onion: 100 g
  - garlic: 10 g
  - olive_oil: 15 g
  - soy_sauce: 10 g
  - sugar: 5 g
  - chili_flakes: 2 g
  - coriander: 5 g
  - black_pepper: 1 g

=== Carrot Salad ===
Folder  : carrot_salad
Cuisine : Asian
Servings: 2
Tags    : vegetarian, appetizer, quick, no-cook

Description:
1. Julienne carrots and slice cucumber into thin half-moons.
2. Mince garlic finely. Roughly chop fresh coriander.
3. In a large bowl, whisk together sesame oil, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sugar, black pepper, and chili flakes to make the dressing.
4. Add carrots, cucumber, and garlic to the bowl. Toss well until evenly coated.
5. Transfer to a serving plate. Top with roasted sesame seeds and fresh coriander before serving.

Ingredients:
  - carrot: 200 g
  - cucumber: 100 g
  - garlic: 5 g
  - coriander: 8 g
  - sesame_oil: 10 g
  - soy_sauce: 15 g
  - rice_vinegar: 10 g
  - sugar: 5 g
  - black_pepper: 1 g
  - chili_flakes: 2 g
  - roasted_sesame_seeds: 10 g

=== Mackerel Fish Cake with Tomato Sauce ===
Folder  : fish_cake_tomato
Cuisine : Vietnamese
Servings: 2
Tags    : main course, seafood, quick

Description:
1. Drain and flake canned mackerel into a bowl. Add one beaten egg, chopped coriander, black pepper, and chili flakes. Mix until combined, then shape into small round patties about 1.5 cm thick.
2. Heat vegetable oil in a skillet over medium heat. Pan-fry fish cakes for 3–4 minutes per side until golden brown. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same skillet, sauté minced garlic and diced onion for 2 minutes until softened.
4. Add diced tomato and soy sauce; cook for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes break down into a saucy consistency.
5. Return fish cakes to the pan and simmer in the sauce for 3 minutes.
6. Serve hot over steamed rice, garnished with fresh coriander.

Ingredients:
  - canned_mackerel: 200 g
  - tomato: 200 g
  - eggs: 1 / 55 g
  - garlic: 10 g
  - onion: 80 g
  - coriander: 8 g
  - soy_sauce: 10 g
  - chili_flakes: 2 g
  - black_pepper: 1 g
  - vegetable_oil: 15 g

=== Japanese Curry ===
Folder  : japanese_curry
Cuisine : Japanese
Servings: 2
Tags    : main course, hearty, family-friendly

Description:
1. Cut chicken breast into bite-sized pieces and season lightly with salt and black pepper.
2. Heat vegetable oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Brown the chicken for 3–4 minutes, then set aside.
3. In the same pot, sauté sliced onion until golden, about 8 minutes.
4. Add garlic and ginger; cook 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Return chicken to the pot. Add carrots and potatoes, then pour in 600 ml water. Bring to a boil and skim any foam.
6. Reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes until vegetables are just tender.
7. Break curry roux blocks into the pot and stir until fully dissolved. Simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens.
8. Serve over steamed rice.

Ingredients:
  - chicken_breast: 300 g
  - onion: 150 g
  - carrot: 150 g
  - potato: 200 g
  - curry_roux: 80 g
  - garlic: 10 g
  - ginger: 5 g
  - vegetable_oil: 15 g
  - rice: 200 g
  - salt: 0.25 tsp
  - black_pepper: 0.25 tsp

=== Mala Noodle ===
Folder  : mala_noodle
Cuisine : Chinese
Servings: 2
Tags    : spicy, main course, quick

Description:
1. Cook noodles according to package instructions until just tender. Drain and rinse briefly with cold water; set aside.
2. Blanch bok choy in the same boiling water for 30 seconds; drain.
3. In a small bowl, stir together mala paste, soy sauce, and sesame oil. Loosen with 2 tablespoons of the noodle cooking water.
4. Divide noodles between two bowls. Pour the mala sauce over and toss to coat.
5. Top with bok choy, spring onion, roasted sesame seeds, and chili flakes. Serve immediately.

Ingredients:
  - noodles: 200 g
  - mala_paste: 30 g
  - bok_choy: 100 g
  - garlic: 10 g
  - sesame_oil: 10 g
  - soy_sauce: 15 g
  - roasted_sesame_seeds: 8 g
  - spring_onion: 20 g
  - chili_flakes: 3 g

=== Stir-Fried Okra with Egg ===
Folder  : okra_egg
Cuisine : Chinese
Servings: 2
Tags    : main course, vegetarian, quick

Description:
1. Trim the tops from the okra and slice diagonally into 2 cm pieces. Pat dry to reduce stickiness.
2. Heat sesame oil in a wok over high heat. Add minced garlic and stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add okra and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes until bright green and just tender, but still with a slight bite.
4. Push okra to the sides of the wok. Crack eggs into the centre and scramble until just set.
5. Toss everything together. Add soy sauce and oyster sauce; stir-fry for 1 more minute.
6. Garnish with sliced spring onion and serve over steamed rice.

Ingredients:
  - okra: 200 g
  - eggs: 2 / 110 g
  - garlic: 10 g
  - sesame_oil: 10 g
  - soy_sauce: 15 g
  - oyster_sauce: 15 g
  - spring_onion: 20 g

=== Red Bean Sweet Soup ===
Folder  : red_bean_sweet_soup
Cuisine : Chinese
Servings: 4
Tags    : dessert, vegetarian, make-ahead, gluten-free

Description:
1. Rinse red beans and soak in cold water for 6–8 hours (or overnight). Drain.
2. Place soaked beans in a pot with 1.2 litres of fresh water and the pandan leaves. Bring to a boil.
3. Skim any foam, then reduce heat to low. Simmer uncovered for 60–75 minutes until beans are soft and some have split open.
4. Add rock sugar and stir until fully dissolved. Simmer for a further 10 minutes.
5. Remove pandan leaves. Ladle into bowls and drizzle with coconut milk before serving.
6. Can be served warm, at room temperature, or chilled.

Ingredients:
  - red_beans: 200 g
  - rock_sugar: 80 g
  - coconut_milk: 100 g
  - pandan: 3 leaves

=== Sushi Bake ===
Folder  : sushi_bake
Cuisine : Japanese
Servings: 4
Tags    : main course, family-friendly, make-ahead, baked

Description:
1. Cook sushi rice according to package instructions. While still hot, fold in rice vinegar, sugar, and salt. Spread evenly into a greased 20×30 cm baking dish.
2. Preheat oven to 200 °C (390 °F).
3. In a bowl, combine shredded imitation crab, cream cheese, Japanese mayo, and sriracha. Mix until smooth.
4. Spread the crab mixture evenly over the rice layer.
5. Sprinkle furikake generously over the top.
6. Bake for 15–20 minutes until the top is golden and bubbling.
7. Remove from oven and sprinkle with another layer of furikake.
8. Serve scooped onto individual nori sheets and roll to eat.

Ingredients:
  - sushi_rice: 400 g
  - rice_vinegar: 30 g
  - sugar: 15 g
  - salt: 5 g
  - imitation_crab: 200 g
  - cream_cheese: 80 g
  - japanese_mayo: 40 g
  - sriracha: 15 g
  - furikake: 20 g
  - nori: 10 g

=== Indonesian Tempeh Rice ===
Folder  : tempeh_rice
Cuisine : Indonesian
Servings: 2
Tags    : main course, vegetarian, quick

Description:
1. Slice tempeh into thin strips or small cubes. Pat dry with paper towel.
2. Heat vegetable oil in a wok over medium-high heat. Fry tempeh until golden and crisp on all sides, about 5–7 minutes. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same wok, sauté minced garlic and diced onion for 2 minutes until fragrant.
4. Add cooked rice and stir-fry, breaking up any clumps, for 2 minutes.
5. Return tempeh to the wok. Add soy sauce and chili flakes; toss everything together for 2–3 minutes until well combined and heated through.
6. Garnish with sliced spring onion and serve hot.

Ingredients:
  - tempeh: 200 g
  - rice: 200 g
  - garlic: 10 g
  - onion: 80 g
  - soy_sauce: 20 g
  - chili_flakes: 3 g
  - vegetable_oil: 15 g
  - spring_onion: 20 g
